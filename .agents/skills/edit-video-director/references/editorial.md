# Editorial and motion-text direction

Establish audience, purpose, length, format, language, mood, source constraints, reference and music. Infer ordinary missing choices. Never shorten a factual explanation until it becomes misleading.

Interview/presenter: retain complete thoughts, breathing and reactions; compare repeated takes, build story beats before shortening. Social: establish the actual question/problem immediately and deliver the promised payoff. Horror: use anticipation, holds, negative space, restrained sound accents and slow reveals; avoid rapid strobe. Smooth: let motion finish, keep readable holds and subtle grades. Comedy: preserve setup, pause, reaction and punchline; not all silence is waste.

| Style | Treatment | Default motion |
| --- | --- | --- |
| clean | White, subtle edge | Fade |
| bold | Yellow, heavy weight | Pop |
| smooth | Warm white, light edge | Upward slide/fade |
| horror | Bone white, dark red edge | Slow blur reveal |
| neon | Cyan, soft glow | Fade |
| caption | White bold, dark outline | None |

Use one title style plus one caption style per video. One idea per card. Short Thai phrases or 2–8 English words are good title starting points, not hard limits. Prefer 1–2 lines; split long copy into timed cues. Keep dialogue captions visually stable, allow enough reading time, and inspect on a small screen. Position titles clear of faces, captions and interface overlays.

Thai: preserve combining marks, insert semantic line breaks, avoid excessive tracking. Review tone marks, names, dates, amounts and negatives. Automatic wrap is glyph-safe fallback, not word segmentation. For bilingual captions use Thai then English on separate lines, reviewed translation and longer holds. Typewriter is for short titles; it reveals combining-safe clusters, not inferred Thai words.

Check the full draft for continuity, exact cut boundaries, title entrances/holds/exits, cropped gestures, quiet/loud speech and ending. Record fixes. Do not claim listening if no audio playback occurred.

Example briefs:
- ใช้ edit-video-director ตัดคลิปให้เหลือ 45 วินาที แนวตั้ง สนุก กระชับ ข้อความ bold เฉพาะคำสำคัญ ซับไทยอ่านง่าย เพลงตามไฟล์แนบ
- ทำตัวอย่างหนังหลอน 30 วินาที ขึ้น “คืนนี้...อย่ารับสาย” แบบ ghost ค่อย ๆ เผย ไม่ใช้ไฟกระพริบเร็ว
- Create a smooth 60-second landscape edit with reviewed Thai/English captions, restrained slide titles and supplied music under speech.
